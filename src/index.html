How It Works

Getting your idea off the ground is as easy as one, two, three.

1. Submit Your Spark

Use our simple form to articulate the problem you're solving and your unique solution. Don't worry about perfection, just get it out there!

2. Gather Feedback

Your idea is shared with our community of student innovators. Receive constructive comments, likes, and validation for your concept.

3. Find Collaborators

Connect with other students who are passionate about your idea. Build a team with diverse skills to bring your vision to life.
